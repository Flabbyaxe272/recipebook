# Cottage Pie

__Alternative Names__: Shepherd's Pie

__Original Source__: Farris Fam

__Servings per Recipe__: 6 bowls

__Description__: People in the family fight about how it should be called.
Cottage Pie is the same thing as Shepherd's Pie with beef rather than lamb.
This is a shortcut recipe.

***

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- Frozen veggie mix (blend of carrots, corn, and peas)
- Mashed potatoes
- Salt, pepper
- &frac14; tsp garlic powder (or 1 tsp fresh garlic)
- &frac14; tsp onion powder (or frac12; real onion)

Optional but high impact:

- Worcestershire sauce
- Soy sauce (tiny bit)
- Ketchup or tomato paste
- Butter or milk (for potatoes)
- Cheese (if you want a golden top)

***

## Instructions

1. Brown the beef, adding a dash of salt and pepper.
2. Add garlic and onion and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Add frozen veggies (no need to thaw), as well as the extra Worcestershire
   and or ketchup or soy sauce.
4. If it's dry, add a splash of beef broth. If it's too wet, add some flour.
5. Dallop mashed potatoes on top, spreading it around until the top is covered.
   Top with cheese if adding that.
6. Bake for 15 minutes at 375&deg;. Keeping it in the oven, turn it on broil
   for 5 minutes. Watch it closely as to not let it burn.
7. Take out and let it rest for about 5-10 minutes.
