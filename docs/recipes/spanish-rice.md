# Spanish Rice

__Original Source__: Wassom Cookbook

__Servings per Recipe__:

__Description__: An amazing recipe that pairs wonderfully with [Chicken Enchiladas](wassom-chickaladas.md). 

***

## Ingredients

- 1 &frac12; c. rice
- &frac14; c. oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- &frac12; tsp garlic, pressed
- 2 c. chicken broth
- 1 c. water
- &frac12; Tbsp cumin
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 8 oz can tomato sauce

***

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add rice and onion, and saute, 
   stirring frequently, until rice is light golden brown, about 5-7 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Stir and cook for 
   30 seconds, just until fragrant — pull it off heat quickly if the garlic 
   starts to darken.
3. Add chicken broth, water, and tomato sauce. Stir once to combine, then 
   bring to a boil.
4. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 20-30 minutes, until rice is 
   tender and liquid is absorbed. Do not stir or lift the lid while it cooks.
5. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a 
   fork before serving.
