# Dad's Smokey Cheeseball

__Original Source__: Dad Farris

Time to Make: Several days[^few-days]

__Servings per Recipe__: 1 Cheeseball

__Description__: A Farris family staple — requested every Christmas without
fail. This cheeseball brings sharp cheddar and a deep smokiness that cuts
through the other holiday spreads, and it disappears faster than anything
else on the table.

***

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- &frac14; tsp salt
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 chunk (about 1-2 oz) each of smoked cheddar & sharp cheddar[^cheese-quality]
- *For extra heat: add a dash of cayenne pepper, or a chunk of buffalo wing
  cheese*
- For extra smoke: a dash of smoked paprika

***

## Instructions

1. Allow the cream cheese to raise to room temperature.[^overnight]
2. Melt down the chunks of smoked cheddar and sharp cheddar.[^microwave]
3. Whip all ingredients together in a standing mixer on high for more than
   two minutes. Adjust cheeses to your preferred taste.
4. Chill for 2+ hours, or until firm.
5. With gloves on, form into a ball and roll in shredded sharp cheddar.
6. Chill overnight in the fridge for best effect.
7. Serve with crackers, spreading with a knife. ENJOY!

[^overnight]: Overnight is best, but at least a few hours.

[^microwave]: I haven't found a way to melt cheese quicker than the
microwave.

[^few-days]: This is a recipe that takes a few days, as you need to let
cheese warm up, chill down, and chill some more overnight, so plan it out at
least a few days in advance.

[^cheese-quality]: But the higher quality the cheese is, the better flavor
will come out.
