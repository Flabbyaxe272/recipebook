# About

I'm Justin, the curator of this recipe book site.
This recipebook was built to share among my family, the Farris' and their
extended family and friends.

Most of these recipes are tried and true to be favorites in the family.
That being said, they are more focused for flavor than diets, so most
are not very healthy 😅 There are some recipes here that are more focused
on couples that can easily double for small families.

On the technical side, this is a markdown site ran using Material for MkDocs.
It's built on the repo for this site, found [here on Github](https://github.com/Flabbyaxe272/recipebook).
For issues, please reach out to me at my email: [justin@farrisfam.org](mailto:justin@farrisfam.org).
